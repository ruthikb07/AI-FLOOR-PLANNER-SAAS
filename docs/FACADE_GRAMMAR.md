### Phase C scale decision and illustrative dimensions

**Use 3.8 m** for the model’s residential floor-to-floor spacing. The aerial reference shows the proportions of stacked floor bands, glazed bays and podium relative to the tower mass. Oblique perspective, unequal apparent distances and an unconfirmed tower/elevation match prevent an independent floor height from that view. The developer’s published metric applies to Tower E. Using it in all three demonstration towers is an illustrative design decision supported by that reference figure. The 1.42 m and 2.40 m balcony plan labels guide the modeled depth contrast. ## Build route

| Route | Strengths | Realistic ceiling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Procedural, dimension-driven Three.js | Rapid iteration after a dimension changes. Repeated bays and neighbors suit instancing, and component identities follow directly from the spec. | Convincing architectural visualization at street and mid distance. Close-ups will expose repeat patterns and simple junctions unless we author detailed moulding, frames and materials. That detail raises runtime authoring and bundle cost. |
| Blender-authored, compressed GLB with baked AO | Hand-authored slab nosings, stone and plaster joints, window reveals, railings, curated interiors and site details. PBR materials and local ambient occlusion (AO) carry surface depth without an extra full-screen AO pass. | Portfolio-quality browser views are plausible at the hero, street and facade angles with sufficient reference coverage and authored detail. Glass refraction, interior bounce light, weathering and unseen elevations will remain approximations. A browser render will differ from a photograph. |

**Recommend the Blender-authored GLB.** Its close-range detailing addresses the visible wall, balcony and glazing issues. Keep Three.js for cameras, live lighting and animation, and use the existing procedural streetscape techniques where repeatable background assets help. Bake contact AO within geometry that moves together, and reserve any fixed lightmaps for static surroundings. Use live HDR reflections, sun shadows and emissive fixtures for the day-to-evening change. Preserve stable component IDs, floor indices, structural phases, final local transforms and an editable source spec. Merge or instance only as far as those component identities and movement allow. Deliver a repeatable model export from the spec. [Blender baking](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.0/render/cycles/baking.html) · [glTF material schema](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF/blob/main/specification/2.0/schema/material.schema.json) · [Three.js GLTFLoader](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/GLTFLoader.html)

## Phase C and D design requirements

- **Ready gate.** Start loading the HDR and scoped GLB at the lazy hero boundary. Decode the required model and textures, prepare the HDR environment and configure material, lighting and clipping options. Await `renderer.compileAsync` for the actual first-view and reachable evening/section shader variants. Warm up the relevant render passes and first texture uploads before setting the ready flag. Check first-use lighting and section transitions in a fresh browser context. [Three.js renderer compile and texture APIs](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/WebGLRenderer)
- **Floor motion.** Disable automatic shadow-map refresh and decouple shadow invalidation from the floor transform loop. Cache the sun shadow at stable poses. During motion, use local AO or floor-relative contact shading and suppress visibly stale moving-caster shadows if needed. Refresh at meaningful settled pose or lighting changes. Verify smooth motion and count the shadow pass separately when profiling.
- **Payload and render budgets.** Keep total first-load welcome JavaScript under **400 kB gzip**, including imported loader and decoder/worker JavaScript used on that path. Lazy-load the model and loaders, use Meshopt or Draco for geometry and KTX2 for textures, and keep the GLB under **15 MB**. Target **under 150 draws** and **under 600,000 triangles** in the final view. Check direct and lazy imports, tests and build output before removing the apparently unused `CinematicWelcomeScene`. Phase A’s chunk inventory already excluded that file, so track the actual bytes saved through import and bundle changes. [Phase A bytes and draw counts](PERF_BASELINE.md) · [Khronos KTX2 extension](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF/tree/main/extensions/2.0/Khronos/KHR_texture_basisu)
- **Benchmark evidence.** Identify the browser-selected GPU and graphics backend on every performance result. Phase A used **Intel UHD Graphics through ANGLE/D3D11**. For the final Part 2 benchmark, run the same production path on Intel UHD and **NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050** with matching browser, viewport, DPR, scene and interaction sequence. Confirm the WebGL renderer string for each run. Phase B gathered reference evidence only. [Baseline environment and method](PERF_BASELINE.md)
