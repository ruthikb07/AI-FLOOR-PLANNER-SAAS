# Deck level and stair ownership

The structural frame elevation comes from the supporting storey. Occupied or terrace decks use the architectural floor registered at their reviewed finished floor level. A terminal roof requires an explicit complete-surface review. Without a registered upper surface or a roof review the diagnostic endpoint returns SURFACE_OWNERSHIP_REVIEW_REQUIRED. Split-level regions, terraces outside the registered outline and stepped soffits require additional reviewed surfaces.

The regional replay preserves the source staircase polygon, both canonical flights, intermediate landing and floor landing. The floor landing belongs to the floor at its registered elevation. Its footprint is restored only inside the stair and floor envelopes. Independent void conflicts block the calculation. The half-rise landing elevation is calculated from the first flight's fraction of the full riser count. These components remain geometric records until supporting frames, connections, self-weight and transfer are reconciled.
