# Coupled gravity verification for reviewed rectangular decks

Structural Studio can solve a saved frame and its reviewed rectangular floor and roof decks together from the registered deck view. The localhost endpoint requires matching architecture identity, exactly one reviewed full deck at each frame level and a closed gravity ledger for the remaining frame sources. Stair systems, nonrectangular decks, partial bearings and mixed project configurations remain gated. This is a linear gravity verification result. RCC, optimization and foundation output continue to identify their original evaluation.

The solver reuses the canonical frame. The load assembler excludes each directly meshed slab from the conventional tributary frame loads. Signed DL, SDL and LL pressures come from the saved source ledger and apply to Q4 elements. Frame self-weight and other frame gravity sources retain their original case assignments. Rectangular axes include every original edge element endpoint and refine at a maximum nominal spacing of 500 mm. Boundary stations subdivide existing beams, preserve end releases and remap member loads. Slabs share only the vertical displacement at each bearing. Horizontal membrane gauges remove rigid in-plane motion for flat decks under normal pressure. Unilateral vertical supports release in tension. Each load case and each combination solves its own contact state. Slip demand, cracked stiffness, alternate live-load patterns, eccentric in-plane force transfer, composite T-beam design and construction approval are outside this verification.

A canonical two-storey synthetic frame has a 4 m by 2.5 m occupied floor and terminal roof, both 160 mm thick. A separately specified uniform live load of 3 and 1.5 kN/m² gives 30 and 15 kN. The production HTTP regression checks 45 kN total vertical root reaction within 0.000001 kN, plus equilibrated case results. Each deck also receives 40 kN slab DL and 10 kN finish SDL. The direct worker strip comparison checks four-metre simply supported elastic deflection within 3%, reaction sums within 1e-7 kN and zero support moments within 1e-7 kN m. Full design-load reconciliation and independent whole-building displacement verification are still required before consuming these results in design.


The first bilateral solve exposed a −0.785 kN roof reaction under live load. The compression-only solve balances each physical deck and its two first moments independently within 1e-6 kN or kN m. It removes tensile contacts below −1e-7 kN, re-engages penetrating open contacts beyond 1e-9 m and rejects cycling, excessive iterations or an unbalanced released bearing. The final synthetic roof minimum is below 1e-12 kN in magnitude. A separate cantilever-on-stop benchmark independently verifies open displacement, closed reaction and a combination that recloses. Multi-contact generality and contact-mesh convergence require additional benchmarks.

The synthetic fixture also exercised conventional RCC and foundation design with explicitly synthetic soil, automatic optimization, DXF, IFC, PDF and BBS export. The conventional evaluation reported maximum utilization 0.817, 12.84 m³ net concrete and 1242.77 kg reinforcement. Optimization returned ALREADY_OPTIMAL with no member edits. These results belong to the conventional evaluation, while the coupled result is a separate immutable receipt. Reopening it verifies the content digest and parent evaluation.
