# Concrete Properties source audit

The installed `concreteproperties` 0.8.0 distribution and recovered local clone identify git commit `13bfb7bfd75b265bcd418e2f8ed72106a0362678`. The worker uses the unmodified upstream `ConcreteSection` and section geometry APIs. It supplies its own Indian-code-based concrete and steel material parameters and applies its own explicit pure-axial cap. The foreign design-code wrappers are excluded. [Pinned upstream source](https://github.com/robbievanleeuwen/concrete-properties/tree/13bfb7bfd75b265bcd418e2f8ed72106a0362678)

- The adapter accepts a rectangular section or explicit polygon and actual reinforcement coordinates and areas, which determine circular-bar diameters. It checks bar containment and overlap before building the section. The analytic integration and gross-property calculations use mm, N and MPa. The worker returns kN and kN·m.
- The worker calls upstream ultimate bending, axial–moment interaction, biaxial moment contours and optional moment–curvature analysis. A signed ray intersects the sampled biaxial boundary at the exact concurrent axial force. The contour approximation and selected material laws require separate validation for each intended scope.
- The daemon uses bounded LRU caches. Section identity includes both material strengths, rectangular dimensions or polygon, each exact bar position and area. Contour identity also includes exact axial force and angular resolution. The bar polygon resolution, material laws and upstream solver tolerances are fixed by the running source and pinned packages.
- The canonical path evaluates one governing demand selected by the RH analytical design per column. It retains the internal design failure when the independent fiber result is lower. Full-combination independent fiber screening, arbitrary section rotations, biaxial tension and practical detailing acceptance remain open. Arbitrary worker polygons do not establish production circular-column design.
- The material-law choices are traceable to the implemented IS 456:2000 design model and historical benchmarks. The complete provisions, amendments, project applicability and departures require an engineering audit. [BIS Amendment 6](https://services.bis.gov.in/tmp/CED19013804_03062024_1.pdf)
