## Automatic structural regeneration

Structural Studio requests regeneration whenever its canonical building or engineering inputs change, including when a different project opens. The evaluation starts with the generated framing. A failed design triggers global sizing changes, targeted strengthening of remaining failed beams and columns, and an alternative beam-sizing strategy as needed. Each candidate receives a fresh whole-building analysis. The engine keeps the architectural revision, reviewed inputs and selected analysis method fixed, and retains the best analyzed revision. A passing design stops the search. Missing required inputs, unsupported checks and exhausted sizing changes remain visible as blockers. Regeneration history is tied to the selected structural evaluation in the API and JSON report.

The focused structural suites passed 10 of 10 checks. A bungalow passed on the first analysis and stopped with a site-report request when the soil inputs were omitted. A separate two-storey model with deliberately increased floor finish loading started with failures and reached zero under the same regeneration flow. The frozen apartment model went from 170 to 10 major or critical findings over 32 analyzed revisions. The selected model has three beam failures, one column failure, six drift failures and 637 preliminary slab load paths. The test supplied a settlement limit. Raft settlement verification still requires the site subgrade modulus.
