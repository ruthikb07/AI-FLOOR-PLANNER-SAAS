## Measurement convention

- The raw residential outline is the ledger gross area, including modeled voids. Geometric residential built-up is that outline minus openings to the sky. Per-flat built-up uses the canonical unit polygons. Net usable carpet is derived from wall-clipped interior room polygons. Apartment balconies and utility outdoor space are measured separately from carpet. Common circulation, core, services, walls, and voids retain separate ledger buckets.
- The legacy schedule road-width allowance and residential built-up/site ratio are conceptual estimates. The audited `udcprReport.fsiUtilization` reports measured regulatory input quantities separately and reports unverified entitlement or exemptions as null. A 1,200 mm project balcony rule and the modeled 2BHK/core area estimates require jurisdiction-specific review.
- Exact carpet targets remain opt-in at the API acceptance boundary with `requireAreaTargets`. Existing Studio values are advisory and misses are visible in the findings. The bounded search cannot prove feasibility of every target or certify RERA carpet area, FSI, egress, or structural design.
