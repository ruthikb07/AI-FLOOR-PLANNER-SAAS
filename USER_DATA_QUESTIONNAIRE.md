## Preferred reply format

Upload existing office schedules and redacted plans where possible. For missing items, reply **“no office standard yet”**, **“unknown”**, or **“not required”** rather than inventing a value. A professional reviewer can resolve those items during the relevant gate. No client identity, soil, structural loads, reinforcement or BOQ rates are requested for V1 architectural generalization.
